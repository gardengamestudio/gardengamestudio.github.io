Meow Metro Privacy Policy

Effective: November 15, 2026 · 한국어 · 日本語

Garden Game Studio ("we", "the developer") makes the mobile game Meow Metro (Korean title 냥냥 메트로; "the game"). This policy explains what information the game handles and how. If this English version and the Korean version differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. Information we collect directly

We do not collect information that identifies you, such as your name, phone number or email address. The game has no sign-up or login, and we run no servers of our own.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress (stations reached, stars, coins, jelly, upgrades, settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

  • Where: the app's web storage (localStorage) and a copy of the same data in app-private storage (Android SharedPreferences). The copy is used only to restore your progress if the web storage is cleared.
  • The game sells nothing for real money (no in-app purchases). Items in the shop are bought with in-game coins only.
  • If Android auto backup is on, Android may include this on-device data in your Google Account backup. Google manages that backup and we cannot see it.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. You can also erase your progress in the game with Settings → Reset progress.

Ad consent: where consent is required, a consent screen appears on first launch, and you can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. The game does not request ads before your consent status is known.

Usage statistics: where consent is required, statistics are sent only if you agree. You can change this in the same Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy or Google → Ads).

If you buy "Remove ads" with in-game coins, the game stops requesting ads.

4. U.S. state privacy rights

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not receive any of it ourselves. Google, as a service provider for ads and analytics, may process the information listed in section 3. Residents of U.S. states with privacy laws (such as California) may ask Google to access or delete information it holds, using the tools described in Google's privacy policy, and can opt out of personalized ads by resetting or deleting the advertising ID as described above. For anything else, contact us at the address below.

5. Children

The game is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Retention and deletion

We do not keep any personal information about players. Data on your device can be deleted as described in section 2.

7. Your rights

You can ask each third-party service to access or delete information it processed, using the methods in its policy. For other questions, please contact us below.
