Cat Landlord Privacy Policy

Effective: November 22, 2026 · 한국어 · 日本語

Garden Game Studio ("we", "the developer") makes the mobile game Cat Landlord (Korean title 냥생역전; "the game"). This policy explains what information the game handles and how. If this English version and the Korean version differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. Information we collect directly

The game has no sign-up or login, and we run no game servers. We do not ask for your name, phone number or email address to play. The one exception is a feedback email you choose to send.

If you use Settings → Send feedback, your email app sends us your email address, what you wrote, and the app version, current chapter and device information (browser information string) that the game fills in for you. Whether to send it is up to you. We use these emails only to reply and to fix problems.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress (money, story progress, decorations, settings, items bought with in-game money) is stored on your device, and your full save is never sent to us automatically. If you send a feedback email, the app version, chapter and device information in section 1 are included.

  • Where: the app's web storage (localStorage) and a copy of the same data in app-private storage (Android SharedPreferences). The copy is used only to restore your progress if the web storage is cleared.
  • The game sells nothing for real money (no in-app purchases). If a purchase record from an earlier version of the game, received through Google Play, is still on your device, we leave it as it is and create no new ones.
  • If Android auto backup is on, Android may include this on-device data in your Google Account backup. Google manages that backup and we cannot see it.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. You can also erase your progress with Settings → Reset progress ("Remove ads" and the "Lucky Cat statue" bought with in-game money are kept).

4. U.S. state privacy rights

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not receive any of it ourselves except the feedback emails you choose to send. Google, as a service provider for ads and analytics, may process the information listed in section 3. Residents of U.S. states with privacy laws (such as California) may ask Google to access or delete information it holds, using the tools described in Google's privacy policy, and can opt out of personalized ads by resetting or deleting the advertising ID as described above. For anything else, contact us at the address below.

5. Children

The game is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Retention and deletion

We do not keep game saves on any server. The email address, message, and attached app version, chapter and device information in feedback emails are used only to answer and to fix problems, and are deleted within 90 days after we finish handling them. Data on your device can be deleted as described in section 2.

7. Your rights

You can ask each third-party service to access or delete information it processed, using the methods in its policy. For other questions, please contact us below.
